# Brennick

**Last updated:** 2026-10-16
**Source(s):** Session 1.md

## Summary
Brennick is a dwarf mercenary at the Hearth and Hollow in [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]] who challenges patrons to arm wrestling and drinking contests. He lost an arm-wrestling match to [[rowan-mercer|Rowan]] 2-1.

## Details
Bet 5 gp on the match; after losing, Rowan returned 4 of the 5 gp to him, which Brennick then tipped to the innkeeper, [[mara-thorne|Mara Thorne]]. Session handwritten notes give his name as "Brannack Holt" — possibly a fuller name for the same person (the Fathom-transcribed portion only ever calls him "Brennick"). **Flagged, unconfirmed:** that surname would make him a namesake, at least, of [[calder-holt|Calder Holt]], Rowan's friend back in Bellweather — no stated connection in source, don't assume one.
