# Linus

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Source(s):** Session 2.md

## Summary
[[seamus|Seamus]]'s companion at the river camp north of the hidden trail outside [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]], met in Session 2. He did most of the talking with the party and was the friendlier of the two, waving them off as they left.

## Details
**Appearance:** same as Seamus: taller than average, dark shaggy hair, dark green cloak, patched leather armor.

**What he told the party:** they came from Bristol Hold and are going to Oakhaven for the concert in about two days. They've been camped for about half a day, waiting for a hermit friend who's out hunting. He insisted there's no trail and the river is fine, and when he pointed at it, it was flowing normally toward Oakhaven again. He had never heard of any missing children, said the torn corner of their Oakhaven map was "just a legend," and asked whether Oakhaven has a brothel.

**The map:** their map of Oakhaven was missing its top-right corner. [[tavian-runewick|Tav]] restored it with his magic stapler and added his maker's mark. Linus thanked him and said their buddy would be "thrilled."
