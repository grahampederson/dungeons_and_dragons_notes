# Elira Runewick

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** Tavian_Runewick.md

## Summary
Elira Runewick is [[tavian-runewick|Tav Runewick]]'s older sister — the wizard who followed the family tradition he switched away from — and the person who pushes him to be rigorous.

## Details
Brilliant, disciplined, skeptical, and affectionate, Elira is completely immune to Tav's bullshit. She constantly challenges his methods: "You tested it twice." / "It worked twice." / "That isn't the same thing." She's often the first person Tav tells about a strange discovery, because he knows she won't dismiss him or humor him.
