# Mae Vanderbuilt

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** Jaecron Daughtry.md

## Summary
Mae Vanderbuilt was [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron Daughtry]]'s lover — a chaotic wildcard who lit up a room and pushed him out of his comfort zone. She is deceased.

## Details
Little else about her is documented yet beyond her relationship to Jaecron and her death, which is the inciting event driving Jaecron's flight from his hometown: her father, [[alister-vanderbuilt|Alister Vanderbuilt]], holds Jaecron responsible for it.
