# Jaecron Daughtry

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** Jaecron Daughtry.md

## Summary
Jaecron Daughtry is a pampered-upbringing rogue-ish adventurer who fled his hometown to escape trial or death at the hands of [[alister-vanderbuilt|Alister Vanderbuilt]] after the death of his lover, [[mae-vanderbuilt|Mae Vanderbuilt]]. He values individuality above social norms, splits his time between homebody comforts and sneaking out to mix with townsfolk, and is driven by a need to test his own limits.

## Details
At home, Jaecron is happiest alone — playing banjo, reading mystical or religious texts, resetting himself by following his own whims. Away from home he misses his books and armchair by a fire. He'd recognize anything made by Daughtry Arms, his family's business, anywhere.

Raised pampered with strict parents, he developed a strong urge to go "against the grain" and carries guilt/shame over the inequality he's seen in the world. His core belief: everyone can and should be completely themselves, even against social norms — he actively encourages weirdness in others.

He's driven by completing goals and testing what he's capable of. For fun: reading, relaxing, playing banjo, and sneaking out to drink with commonfolk at pubs. Given two free weeks with nothing to do, he'd disappear to a remote spot within 50 miles — mountains, ocean, or even a cozy swamp — to be hard to find. He's into sweets, will do drugs, and is always looking to bum a smoke or some loose leaf for his pipe.

He became an adventurer by necessity: he had to flee to avoid trial or being killed by Alister Vanderbuilt. Music has been part of his life since childhood, largely through time spent with his grandfather.

His weakness is chasing a good time — sneaking out, drinking too much — which repeatedly lands him around sketchy places and sketchy people. Despite years around music, he's oddly bad at percussion; he can't focus long enough to play anything percussive. He's afraid he'll never get to see every corner of the world, even though he'd claim to be fine without it — he just wants to be sure he's not missing something.
