# Varus

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Source(s):** Session 2.md

## Summary
The apparent leader of a three-person group of treasure hunters ([[varus|Varus]], [[sarah|"Sarah"]], [[dunkirk|Dunkirk]]) whom the party found trying to break into [[the-watchtower|the watchtower]] in Session 2. He was smooth and talkative, unlike his companions.

## Details
**Name:** spelled several ways in the transcript ("Bear," "Varus," "Varys," "Vera"). The DM clarified the leader is the man, so this entry uses **Varus** until the table confirms.

**At the tower:** said he'd "got word of a disappearing watchtower that has to have some awesome treasure in it." He apologized for Sarah's hot temper and her earlier run-in in Oakhaven. [[rowan-mercer|Rowan]] persuaded the trio (20) to help get into the tower, but the entrance was caved in. When the party climbed in from the top, the trio stayed below and eventually left, telling [[naia-stillwater|Naia]] "we're not here for kids, we're here for treasure."

**Flagged:** wears the same style of gear as [[seamus|Seamus]] and [[linus|Linus]] at the river camp, according to Naia's Perception check. No connection stated yet.
