# Seamus

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Source(s):** Session 2.md

## Summary
One of two men camped by the river north of the hidden trail outside [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]], met in Session 2. With his companion [[linus|Linus]], he says he's from Bristol Hold and is headed to Oakhaven for the upcoming concert. He was the target of most of the party's pranks and ended the encounter angry. "We'll find you at the concert."

## Details
**Appearance:** taller than average, dark shaggy hair, dark green cloak, patched leather armor. Carried swords and daggers (the DM gave them an Irish lilt).

**The encounter:** went to investigate [[tavian-runewick|Tav]]'s owlbear-noise gadget, stabbed it, and threw up from the stink bomb it released. Cooked the party rabbit stew and shared ale. Kept a close eye on [[naia-stillwater|Naia]] while she snooped at their map. As the party left he noticed his swords and coin purse were missing (Naia stole them). He batted Tav's second stink ball back with a stick, and it landed at the river's edge.

**Claims:** says the pair are waiting at camp for a third "buddy," a loner/hermit who likes hunting, before going to the concert in about two days. Denied knowing anything about missing children. [[rowan-mercer|Rowan]]'s Insight (14) read them as sincere that they hadn't noticed anything strange.

**Flagged:** dressed like the treasure hunters at [[the-watchtower|the watchtower]] ([[varus|Varus]], [[sarah|"Sarah"]], [[dunkirk|Dunkirk]]). No connection stated yet.
