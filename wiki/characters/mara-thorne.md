# Mara Thorne

**Last updated:** 2026-10-16
**Source(s):** Session 1.md

## Summary
Mara Thorne is the innkeeper at the Hearth and Hollow in [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]], where the party first converged. **Not to be confused with [[mara-mercer|Mara Mercer]]**, Rowan's mother — a different character who shares only a first name.

## Details
Runs the Hearth and Hollow. Owns a painting — a portrait of her mother — that hangs crooked no matter how it's fixed; she's resigned to it and declined to sell it to Tav when he asked. See [[the-crooked-painting|The Crooked Painting]]. Received a 4 gp tip from [[brennick|Brennick]] after he lost an arm-wrestling bet to [[rowan-mercer|Rowan]].
