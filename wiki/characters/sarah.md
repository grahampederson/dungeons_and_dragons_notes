# "Sarah"

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Source(s):** Session 1.md, Session 2.md

## Summary
A hot-headed woman in [[varus|Varus]]'s treasure-hunting trio. She first appeared in Session 1 bullying a wagon driver in [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]] about the watchtower, and the party met her again at [[the-watchtower|the watchtower]] in Session 2.

## Details
**Name:** heard as "Sarah," "Serra," "Sarus," and "Sarasaris" in the transcript, so the spelling isn't settled. The entry title is in quotes for that reason.

**Session 1:** with [[dunkirk|a large aggressive man]], demanded that a man in a wagon tell them where the watchtower was. When he said Oakhaven never had one, they broke his wagon wheel. (In that scene she was the one calming her partner down.)

**Session 2:** at the tower she called him "that prick of an old man" who "obviously" lied, which is why she "had to yell at him and threaten him." She turned down [[tavian-runewick|Tav]]'s "message recorder" gift, correctly guessing it was a stink bomb ("that's a bomb, you idiot"). She refused to climb the tower.

**Flagged, unconfirmed:** on seeing the trio, [[naia-stillwater|Naia]] had "a weird feeling, like something seems familiar" (Perception 3). Given the name heard here, she might be [[sera-voss|Sera Voss]], Naia's former fellow initiate turned mercenary rival, but nothing in source confirms it. This is a guess to watch for, not an established fact.
