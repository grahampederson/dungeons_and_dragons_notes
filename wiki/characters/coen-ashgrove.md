# Coen Ashgrove

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** My backstory hurts - Graham.md, Naia Stillwater.md

## Summary
Coen Ashgrove is a fellow initiate at the Cistern Monastery and [[naia-stillwater|Naia Stillwater]]'s closest friend — loud, clumsy, and cheerfully bad at stillness meditation, the opposite of her discipline.

## Details
Coen never took to Open Hand training the way Naia did and remains a middling fighter, more likely to trip over his stance than land a clean strike. He's the one who taught Naia to laugh again after the storm that killed her parents.

During the worst storm surge in a generation (when Naia was 16), Coen got pinned under a collapsed support beam in the flooding lower hall of the monastery. Naia went back in and got him out — not through disciplined technique but raw panic and refusal to let go, an event that cracked open her whole worldview about stillness and control. Coen never lets her forget she wasn't calm that night, and needles her (gently, constantly) out of her more self-serious moments.

Not currently confirmed to be traveling with the party — Naia carries his name as a compass point for hard decisions, and is afraid of never getting to grow old with people who knew her before, him included.
