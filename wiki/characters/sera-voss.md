# Sera Voss

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Source(s):** My backstory hurts - Graham.md, Naia Stillwater.md, Session 2.md

## Summary
Sera Voss is a former fellow initiate of [[naia-stillwater|Naia Stillwater]] at the Cistern Monastery, now a rival adventurer/mercenary, and someone Naia has a raw, ongoing conflict with.

## Details
Sera trained alongside Naia and [[coen-ashgrove|Coen Ashgrove]] for years — talented, ambitious, and openly contemptuous of what she saw as the monastery's "wasted" isolation. She left the monastery five years before Naia did, publicly breaking with [[master-ithren|Master Ithren]] over a philosophical dispute: that the monks' pursuit of inner stillness was fear of the world dressed up as virtue.

Naia took it personally, partly because she privately half-agrees with pieces of Sera's argument, especially after living through the flood. The two have crossed paths twice since as adventurers, both times circling the same job or reward, both times ending in an argument that had nothing to do with the actual work. Sera isn't a villain — she's sharp, capable, and not entirely wrong about Naia's blind spots, which is exactly what makes her hard to be around.

**Possible sighting (Session 2, unconfirmed):** a hot-headed woman in a treasure-hunting trio at [[the-watchtower|the watchtower]], heard in the transcript as "Sarah"/"Serra," gave [[naia-stillwater|Naia]] an unexplained sense of familiarity. Nothing confirms it's Sera, so it's tracked on [[sarah|her own page]] for now.
