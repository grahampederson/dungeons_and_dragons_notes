# Professor Mirabel Runewick

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** Tavian_Runewick.md

## Summary
Professor Mirabel Runewick is [[tavian-runewick|Tav Runewick]]'s mother — a former practicing wizard turned academic, warm, formidable, and curious.

## Details
Mirabel recognized Tav's magical talent early and expected him to follow the family wizard tradition. His switch to the School of Applied Thaumaturgy and Mechanica disappointed her, not because she considers his work inferior, but because she'd imagined they might someday share a profession. She's gradually realized they share the same love of discovery, just practiced differently — she tells colleagues Tav "specializes in practical applications of recovered thaumaturgical technologies"; Tav just says he fixes things.

Married to Professor Aldren Runewick (also a former wizard turned academic; not yet its own entry — see `log.md`).
