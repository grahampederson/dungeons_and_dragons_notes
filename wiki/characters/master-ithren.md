# Master Ithren

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** My backstory hurts - Graham.md, Naia Stillwater.md

## Summary
Master Ithren is the eldest teacher at the Cistern Monastery and [[naia-stillwater|Naia Stillwater]]'s mentor — the monk who found her, named her, and raised her after her parents' deaths.

## Details
Unsentimental and exacting, Ithren has never once told Naia she's talented, but never let another student spar with her without saying "watch her feet" first — his version of pride. He gave Naia her name after her parents' names were lost with their boat.

After the flood in which Naia went back for [[coen-ashgrove|Coen Ashgrove]], Ithren told her "That wasn't your training. That was you." — simultaneously the kindest and most destabilizing thing he'd said to her. When Naia later decided to leave the monastery to learn what happened to her parents, Ithren didn't stop her; he told her the marsh had taught her everything it could about controlling water, but nothing about the ocean that took her parents.

Not currently traveling with the party. Naia still frames hard decisions as "what would Ithren say" — usually right before deciding to do the opposite.
