# Tavian “Tav” Runewick

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** Tavian_Runewick.md

## Summary
Tavian Runewick is a rock gnome artificer and repairman from a respected lineage of wizard families. He switched from the traditional arcane track to the School of Applied Thaumaturgy and Mechanica due to his interest in practical applications of magic. Tav is known for his curiosity, experimentation, and engineering skills, often fixing ancient mechanisms and salvaging ruins. He encounters divine objects and artifacts, which he attempts to understand and improve.

## Details
Tav Runewick comes from a family of wizards and academics. His parents, Aldren and Mirabel Runewick, were practicing wizards before becoming academics, and his older sister Elira successfully followed the family tradition. Tav was expected to follow the same path but switched to the School of Applied Thaumaturgy and Mechanica after discovering his interest in practical applications of magic.

Tav became a traveling artificer, repairman, salvager, and reverse-engineer of ancient magical technology. He is particularly interested in understanding how ancient civilizations solved practical problems and adapting those solutions to modern life. His philosophy is that magic isn't mysterious and that we just haven't figured out how it works yet. He respects scholarship and reads constantly, believing that knowledge is incomplete until it is tested and useful.

Tav encountered an ancient divine object, referred to as Rune Thing™, which he determined wasn't functioning properly. He fixed it, drawing the attention of a divine entity. Tav's response to divine communication is often sarcastic and practical, as he finds it unnecessary and inefficient.

Tav's family home is a comfortable old house full of books, magical curiosities, and academic correspondence. His traveling workshop-wagon has become a second home, although nobody else understands its organizational system. He carries several tools from the battered tool roll Edric Veyne gave him when he was young.

Tav's parents gave him scholarship in magical theory, history, rigorous study, and respect for accumulated knowledge. Master Edric Veyne, a longtime family friend and craftsman, gave him craftsmanship. Tav's philosophy is that scholarship tells us what people know, experimentation tells us whether they're right, and engineering asks what useful thing we can do with it.

Tav likes fixing things and salvaging ruins to find practical solutions to problems. He is cautious with systems he understands but is irresistibly drawn to unknown systems. His greatest weakness is his curiosity combined with confidence that understanding something makes it manageable. He is also bad at recognizing when an explanation has become a lecture.

Tav wants to leave things working better than he found them and doesn't need fame, wealth, or recognition as a great hero. He is afraid he'll never know when to stop looking for the next problem.
