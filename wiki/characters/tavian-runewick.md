# Tavian “Tav” Runewick

**Last updated:** 2023-10-01
**Source(s):** Tavian_Runewick.md

## Summary
Tavian Runewick is a rock gnome artificer and repairman from a respected lineage of wizard families. He is driven by a passion for understanding, building, and repairing ancient magical technology. Tav's curiosity and confidence in his abilities often lead him to tackle unknown systems, sometimes with dangerous consequences. He is deeply curious about the divine object known as Rune Thing™ and is determined to fix it, despite the risks.

## Details
Tav Runewick comes from a family of wizards and academics. His parents, Professors Aldren and Mirabel Runewick, were practicing wizards before becoming academics. His older sister, Elira, successfully followed the family tradition and became a wizard. Tav was expected to follow in their footsteps but switched to the School of Applied Thaumaturgy and Mechanica after discovering his interest in practical applications of magic.

Tav is a traveling artificer, repairman, salvager, and reverse-engineer of ancient magical technology. He is known for his hands-on approach to problem-solving and his belief that magic operates according to principles that can be understood with sufficient information. Tav's philosophy is that scholarship, experimentation, and engineering can explain how systems work, and he is eager to apply this approach to divine magic.

Tav's greatest weakness is his curiosity combined with confidence that understanding something makes it manageable. This trait drives him to investigate unknown systems, sometimes with dangerous consequences. He is deeply curious about the divine object known as Rune Thing™ and is determined to fix it, despite the risks.

Tav's primary motivation is to leave things working better than he found them. He wants to improve people's lives through the use of forgotten knowledge and make it useful again. He is also afraid of never knowing when to stop looking for the next problem, as he worries that he may spend his entire life learning how everything works without ever learning when to put down his tools and simply enjoy what he already has.
