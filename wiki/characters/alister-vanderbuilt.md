# Alister Vanderbuilt

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** Jaecron Daughtry.md

## Summary
Alister Vanderbuilt is [[mae-vanderbuilt|Mae Vanderbuilt]]'s father — a mean, thuggish figure suspected (not confirmed) of gang or criminal-brotherhood affiliation — and the reason [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron Daughtry]] fled his hometown.

## Details
Alister holds Jaecron responsible for Mae's death and is believed to want him dead for it. That threat is what forced Jaecron to leave and become an adventurer, to avoid trial or being killed.
