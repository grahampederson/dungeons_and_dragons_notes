# Grandpa Daughtry

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** Jaecron Daughtry.md

## Summary
Grandpa Daughtry is [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron Daughtry]]'s grandfather, founder of Daughtry Arms, and the person who pushed Jaecron to become better — patient, always has an answer, gives his full attention.

## Details
He taught Jaecron to fight — when to show grace and when to show no mercy — and also taught him to play music. Jaecron would recognize anything made by Daughtry Arms anywhere, a mark of how central Grandpa Daughtry's craft and teaching are to him.
