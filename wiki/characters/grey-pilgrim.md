# The Grey Pilgrim

**Last updated:** 2026-10-16
**Source(s):** Session 1.md

## Summary
The Grey Pilgrim is a mysterious figure carrying a dark walking stick with a silver ring (or gold handle) around it, who independently approached contacts of all four PCs before Session 1 and sent each of them to [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]] — the thread that converged the whole party. His identity and goal are unknown.

## Details
Each PC heard about him secondhand, from a different contact, with slightly different details (possibly the same figure, possibly several matching his description — unconfirmed):
- Told a friend of [[naia-stillwater|Naia]]'s that someone in Oakhaven knows about the flood at the Cistern Monastery.
- Told [[tavian-runewick|Tav]]'s mentor, Edric Veyne, that someone in Oakhaven knows about Tav's Rune Thing™. This instance of him carried a stick with a gold handle.
- Told an old musician that [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron]]'s favorite musician would be performing in Oakhaven.
- Was met by a friend of [[rowan-mercer|Rowan]]'s from his parents' inn in [[bellweather|Bellweather]] (unnamed in the session note — possibly [[garrick-vale|Garrick Vale]] or [[calder-holt|Calder Holt]], unconfirmed), which is why a parcel is waiting for Rowan in Oakhaven.

No PC has met him directly yet as of the end of Session 1.
