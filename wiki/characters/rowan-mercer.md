# Rowan Mercer

**Last updated:** 2026-10-16
**Source(s):** Rowan Mercer.md, Session 1.md

## Summary
Rowan Mercer is a 27-year-old human Fighter, concept "The Collector" — an adventurous, easygoing young man who grew up at his family's inn in [[bellweather|Bellweather]] and left to go experience the kinds of stories he spent his childhood hearing from travelers. His core belief: *nobody does anything great alone.*

## Details
Athletic and friendly, Rowan dresses like a practical traveler (chain mail worn under a weathered coat, not showy armor) and carries a greatsword plus a pouch of small keepsakes — coins, buttons, tokens — each tied to a person he's met or an experience he's had. Signature phrase: "Oh yeah, I know a guy."

Grew up at the Mercer family inn in Bellweather, a crossroads town, surrounded by passing travelers, merchants, and adventurers. At sixteen, a storm stranded a group of travelers at the inn for a night; Rowan shared what he remembers as one of the best nights of his life with one traveler in particular — but by morning he couldn't remember anything about them (name, face, what they talked about). This is the origin of his habit of collecting keepsakes to remember people and moments by. He later noticed an old coin in his collection he has no memory of acquiring — DM hooks "The Forgotten Traveler" and "The Unfamiliar Coin" both center on this unresolved thread.

[[garrick-vale|Garrick Vale]], an adventurer who regularly stayed at the inn, became Rowan's mentor — taught him to travel, prepare for the unexpected, and fight (Rowan has a natural talent for it). Growing up around so many different people also left Rowan constantly picking up useful knowledge and connections, hence "I know a guy."

Rowan left Bellweather to experience the extraordinary things he'd only heard about secondhand, and to become part of those stories himself — not for fame, just to be able to say "Yeah. I was there." His weakness: he tends to follow interesting opportunities without thinking through consequences, which gets him into trouble.

## Session 1 — Oakhaven
Rowan is in [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]] for a parcel waiting for him and wagon parts — his wagon broke down on the road there. The parcel connects to the [[grey-pilgrim|Grey Pilgrim]]: a friend of Rowan's from his parents' inn in [[bellweather|Bellweather]] (unnamed in the session note — possibly [[garrick-vale|Garrick Vale]] or [[calder-holt|Calder Holt]], unconfirmed) ran into the Grey Pilgrim, which is why the parcel ended up waiting for him here.

At the Hearth and Hollow: beat [[brennick|Brennick]], a dwarf mercenary, 2-1 in an arm-wrestling match for a 5 gp bet — gave 4 gp back afterward as a show of good sport, which Brennick then tipped to the innkeeper, [[mara-thorne|Mara Thorne]]. Rowan kept the returned gold coin as a keepsake (fitting his habit — see Details above). Also recognized the Daughtry Arms signet on [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron]]'s short sword — someone carrying that sigil once stopped at the Mercer family inn in Bellweather, though neither of them knows who. See [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron Daughtry]] for the other side of that thread.

**Dire Wolf ambush** (on the trail behind the chapel, part of [[the-missing-children|The Missing Children]]): missed his attack on Dire Wolf 2 but dealt 5 "graze" damage. Later took 6 damage from Dire Wolf 1 (17 → 11 HP), then landed the killing blow on Dire Wolf 2 (22 to hit, 14 damage).
