# Dunkirk

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Source(s):** Session 1.md, Session 2.md

## Summary
A large, older, mostly silent man in [[varus|Varus]]'s treasure-hunting trio. Apparently the aggressive man who broke a wagon wheel in [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]] in Session 1, he was met again at [[the-watchtower|the watchtower]] in Session 2.

## Details
**Name:** called "Dunkirk" by Varus. One transcript line says "Gron," which is probably a mishearing.

**Session 1:** with [[sarah|"Sarah"]], bullied a wagon driver about the watchtower and broke his wheel. He was the more aggressive of the two there.

**Session 2:** mostly stared and said nothing. When [[tavian-runewick|Tav]] made small talk he answered "What's it to you?" and "I'm only here because Varus said I had to help out." He did not climb the tower.
