# Professor Corvin Drell

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12
**Source(s):** Tavian_Runewick.md

## Summary
Professor Corvin Drell is [[tavian-runewick|Tav Runewick]]'s former professor — brilliant, cautious, meticulous, proud, and someone Tav has a running history of conflict with.

## Details
Drell believed Tav was too willing to experiment with poorly understood systems; Tav believed Drell sometimes confused caution with understanding. Both have been proven right at different points: Drell once stopped Tav from attempting something because prior researchers had established it was dangerous — he was correct. Later, Tav restored an ancient mechanism using an approach Drell insisted couldn't work — Tav was correct. Neither has let the other forget either incident. They secretly respect each other and would prefer nobody point that out.
