# The Crooked Painting

**Status:** On Hold
**Last updated:** 2026-10-16
**Source(s):** Session 1.md

## Summary
A painting at the Hearth and Hollow — a portrait of innkeeper [[mara-thorne|Mara Thorne]]'s mother — re-crooks itself no matter how it's fixed, on no predictable schedule. An unnamed old man at the inn has apparently been trying to fix it daily for the past month. The party investigated inconclusively and moved on.

## Details
Tried fixes witnessed or attempted in Session 1: straightening it by hand, nailing it to the wall, nailing it a second time. None held — the old man says he's tried all of this himself daily for a month and it always comes back crooked at the same angle. Recurrence interval is inconsistent: he reports anywhere from 10 minutes to 24 hours between re-crookings.

Mara Thorne confirmed it's a portrait of her mother and declined to sell it when Tav asked. No connection to the [[grey-pilgrim|Grey Pilgrim]] or any other active thread has been established — flagged as a possible hook, not a confirmed one.
