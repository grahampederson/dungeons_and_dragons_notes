# The Missing Children

**Status:** Active
**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Source(s):** Session 1.md, Session 2.md

## Summary
Four children (Mira, Colin, Zeck, and Frank) vanished overnight from [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]]. [[major-deuce|Mayor Major Deuce]] and his assistant [[laurel|Laurel]] offered the party 400 gp to find them. The party found the children's hidden trail and was ambushed by two dire wolves on it; the children themselves had not yet been located by the end of Session 1. In Session 2 the party followed the children's tracks to [[the-watchtower|the watchtower]], found a hidden basement holding a basin that seems to be a portal, and went through it. The children are still not found.

## Details
**The children:** Mira, Colin, Zeck, and Frank. Per the mayor, Mira and a child named Tolan are close friends who visit each other's houses; Zeck and Frank's involvement was guessed as kids-with-crushes-on-them. Take these character notes as provisional — they came out of the DM improvising live and may shift.

**The clue:** the children were overheard (by an uninvolved townsperson) discussing an "old trail" leading to a "watchtower." Search parties and parents couldn't find any such trail from town.

**The discovery:** the party found the trail's entrance hidden behind the chapel — invisible until revealed by a partial eclipse.

**The ambush:** two dire wolves attacked the party on the trail. Both were killed — see the Dire Wolf Ambush note in each party member's entry for individual combat detail. [[tavian-runewick|Tav]]'s Perception caught their breathing in the bushes first; his failed Stealth check triggered the ambush before the party could act.

**Status at session end:** trail secured past the ambush point; children still missing; party had not yet reached the watchtower.

**Session 2 — the watchtower:**
- A detour after a third dire wolf (tracks ending mid-lunge) led to a river camp run by [[seamus|Seamus]] and [[linus|Linus]], who denied knowing about any children. This looked like a dead end for the quest.
- Back on the trail, the children's footprints were still there and still heading the right way. They led up a spiral path to [[the-watchtower|the watchtower]] on a cliff over Oakhaven, which the town's adults say doesn't exist.
- Treasure hunters ([[varus|Varus]], [[sarah|"Sarah"]], [[dunkirk|Dunkirk]]) were already at the tower. They said outright they were there "for treasure, not kids."
- No sign of the children inside the tower. The hidden basement held a basin of upward-flowing water that pulled in [[naia-stillwater|Naia]], and the party followed. Whether the children went the same way is **unknown**.

**Status at end of Session 2:** Active. Party has entered the basin portal under the watchtower; children still missing.
