# The Missing Children

**Status:** Active
**Last updated:** 2026-10-16
**Source(s):** Session 1.md

## Summary
Four children (Mira, Colin, Zeck, and Frank) vanished overnight from [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]]. [[major-deuce|Mayor Major Deuce]] and his assistant [[laurel|Laurel]] offered the party 400 gp to find them. The party found the children's hidden trail and was ambushed by two dire wolves on it; the children themselves had not yet been located by the end of Session 1.

## Details
**The children:** Mira, Colin, Zeck, and Frank. Per the mayor, Mira and a child named Tolan are close friends who visit each other's houses; Zeck and Frank's involvement was guessed as kids-with-crushes-on-them. Take these character notes as provisional — they came out of the DM improvising live and may shift.

**The clue:** the children were overheard (by an uninvolved townsperson) discussing an "old trail" leading to a "watchtower." Search parties and parents couldn't find any such trail from town.

**The discovery:** the party found the trail's entrance hidden behind the chapel — invisible until revealed by a partial eclipse.

**The ambush:** two dire wolves attacked the party on the trail. Both were killed — see the Dire Wolf Ambush note in each party member's entry for individual combat detail. [[tavian-runewick|Tav]]'s Perception caught their breathing in the bushes first; his failed Stealth check triggered the ambush before the party could act.

**Status at session end:** trail secured past the ambush point; children still missing; party had not yet reached the watchtower.
