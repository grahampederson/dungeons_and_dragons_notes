# The Watchtower

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Source(s):** Session 1.md, Session 2.md

## Summary
An old two-story wood-and-stone watchtower on a cliff overlooking all of [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]], at the end of the hidden trail behind the chapel. The missing children were overheard talking about it, but Oakhaven's adults insist no watchtower exists. The party reached it in Session 2, found the missing children's tracks leading up to it, and discovered a hidden basement holding what looks like a magical portal.

## Details
**Getting there:** the hidden trail leads north from behind the chapel. Near the end, the river runs right past the tower's hill. A worn path spirals up the steep hill, and the children's footprints follow it.

**Others looking for it:** in Session 1 the party saw a woman and an aggressive man bullying a wagon driver in Oakhaven over where the watchtower was; they broke his wagon wheel when he said none existed. In Session 2 those two ([[sarah|"Sarah"]] and [[dunkirk|Dunkirk]]), led by [[varus|Varus]], were found at the tower trying to get in, hunting rumored treasure ("all disappearing watchtowers have treasure in them"). They left rather than follow the party inside.

**Exterior:** the ground-floor door is caved in with rubble. A bell at the top (it rings a G note; ringing it did nothing) and a telescope on the roof. No windows on the approach side. The party got in by climbing up to the bell and taking the stairs down.

**Interior (top to bottom):**
- **Upper storage room:** feels a few hundred years old. A crate held rope, ~44 gp, and a potion of healing; a crumbling drawer held tools, a **journal**, and a **metal piece with a marking in the same script as [[tavian-runewick|Tav]]'s Rune Thing™**. A second potion of healing was also found.
- **Main level:** a lived-in common room (table with cups, plates, ~24 gp, star maps) and a bedroom.
- **The journal** (now with [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron]]): entries say the stars are "different than they were," that "the stars are humming a weird melody… quite soothing," and "hopefully we can go home soon. This treasure is taking a toll on me."
- **Hidden basement:** Tav's Rune Thing™ pulsed faster near a corner stone with a matching symbol. Touching it to the stone opened the floor onto stairs down. The basement has runes all around the room, a large stone head at the back, and a basin carved from one solid stone. Its water drips **upward**. In it you can see a dark, cloudy object with something shining in the middle, like a view into another room. [[naia-stillwater|Naia]]'s Shape Water briefly froze the reverse flow into an upward icicle before it dispersed. When she reached for the shiny object she was pulled in, and a thrown rope just fell to the bottom of the basin. The rest of the party joined hands and followed (end of Session 2).

**Unresolved:** who lived here, what the "treasure" is, where the basin leads, and whether the children went through it.
