# Oakhaven

**Last updated:** 2026-10-16
**Source(s):** Session 1.md

## Summary
Oakhaven is the town where the party — [[naia-stillwater|Naia]], [[tavian-runewick|Tav]], [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron]], and [[rowan-mercer|Rowan]] — first converged, each independently drawn there by the mysterious [[grey-pilgrim|Grey Pilgrim]]. Session 1 takes place almost entirely here: at the Hearth and Hollow inn, the mayor's office, and the trail behind the chapel.

## Details
**The Hearth and Hollow** is the inn where the party met — run by innkeeper [[mara-thorne|Mara Thorne]]. Notable features/events there in Session 1:
- [[brennick|Brennick]], a dwarf mercenary, challenges patrons to arm wrestling and drinking contests; Rowan beat him 2-1.
- A crooked painting on the wall — a portrait of Mara Thorne's mother — keeps re-crooking itself no matter how it's fixed or nailed down, on no predictable schedule (anywhere from 10 minutes to 24 hours). See [[the-crooked-painting|The Crooked Painting]].
- A window near the bar that leaked during a storm; Naia repaired it (twice, after accidentally tearing it open again).
- [[clayton|Clayton]], Jaecron's friend, busks there for tips with Jaecron.

**The chapel and the old trail:** the mayor's office sent the party after four missing children ([[the-missing-children|The Missing Children]]). A trail the children had been overheard discussing — leading to a "watchtower" — was invisible to search parties and parents, but the party found its entrance hidden behind the chapel, revealed by a partial eclipse. The party was ambushed by two dire wolves on this trail (both killed) before the session ended.

**The mayor's office:** home to [[major-deuce|Mayor Major Deuce]] and his assistant [[laurel|Laurel]], who handle town business including the missing-children request.
