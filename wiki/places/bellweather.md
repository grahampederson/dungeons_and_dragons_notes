# Bellweather

**Last updated:** 2026-10-16
**Source(s):** Rowan Mercer.md, Session 1.md

## Summary
Bellweather is a small crossroads town regularly passed through by travelers, merchants, and adventurers. It's [[rowan-mercer|Rowan Mercer]]'s hometown and the place he still feels he belongs.

## Details
Home to the Mercer family inn, owned by [[mara-mercer|Mara Mercer]] and her husband Edric Mercer, where Rowan grew up surrounded by travelers' stories. [[garrick-vale|Garrick Vale]] regularly stayed there and became Rowan's mentor. [[calder-holt|Calder Holt]], a longtime friend (and Rowan's main conflict partner), is also from Bellweather.

A storm when Rowan was sixteen stranded a group of travelers at the inn for a night that became the origin of his habit of collecting keepsakes — see [[rowan-mercer|Rowan Mercer]]'s entry for detail ("The Forgotten Traveler" DM hook).

**Flagged, unconfirmed (Session 1):** someone carrying a weapon or piece of gear bearing the Daughtry Arms signet once stopped at the Mercer family inn here — [[rowan-mercer|Rowan]] recognized the sigil on [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron Daughtry]]'s short sword in Oakhaven. Neither PC knows who that visitor was yet; worth tracking as a possible connection between the Mercer and Daughtry sides of the party.
