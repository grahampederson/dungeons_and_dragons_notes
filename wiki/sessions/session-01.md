# Session 1 — Oakhaven

**Date played:** 2026-10-15
**Source(s):** `Campaigns/the_remembered_and_forgotten/sessions/Session 1.md`

## Summary
The party converged on [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]] at the Hearth and Hollow inn,
each independently drawn there by the mysterious [[grey-pilgrim|Grey
Pilgrim]]. After meeting over an arm-wrestling match and a stubbornly
crooked painting, they took on a 400 gp job from the mayor's office to
find four missing children, tracked a hidden trail behind the chapel, and
survived a dire wolf ambush along it.

## Key events
- **Introductions:** [[naia-stillwater|Naia]] (chasing word of the
  monastery flood), [[tavian-runewick|Tav]] (chasing word of his rune
  artifact), [[jaecron-daughtry|Jaecron]] (chasing his favorite musician),
  and [[rowan-mercer|Rowan]] (chasing a parcel and wagon parts) all arrived
  in Oakhaven on separate tips from the same Grey Pilgrim.
- **Arm wrestling:** Rowan beat [[brennick|Brennick]] 2-1 for 5 gp, then
  gave 4 gp back as good sport; Brennick tipped it to [[mara-thorne|Mara
  Thorne]].
- **The crooked painting:** The party investigated a painting that
  re-crooks itself daily. Mara confirmed it's a portrait of her mother and
  not for sale — mystery unresolved, see [[the-crooked-painting]].
- **The job:** [[laurel|Laurel]], assistant to [[major-deuce|Mayor Major
  Deuce]], offered 400 gp to find four missing children (Mira, Colin,
  Frank, and Zeck) — see [[the-missing-children]].
- **The trail:** A partial eclipse revealed a hidden trail behind the
  chapel that the children's own parents couldn't find.
- **Dire wolf ambush:** Tav's Perception caught the ambush, but his failed
  Stealth check tipped it off. Two dire wolves hit Tav and Naia hard in
  round 1; the party turned it around and killed both by round 3.
