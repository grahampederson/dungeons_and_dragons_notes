# Session 2 — The Watchtower

**Date played:** 2026-10-29
**Source(s):** `Campaigns/the_remembered_and_forgotten/sessions/Session 2.md`

## Summary
Picking up right after the dire wolf fight, the party followed a third
wolf's tracks (which ended mid-lunge) to a river camp, where they tricked,
robbed, and then chatted up two green-cloaked men, [[seamus|Seamus]] and
[[linus|Linus]]. Back on the children's trail, the strangeness piled up —
vanished wolf corpses, delayed sounds, a crow that kept coming back — until
they reached the "nonexistent" [[the-watchtower|watchtower]]. Inside, Tav's
Rune Thing™ opened a hidden basement holding a basin of upward-flowing
water; Naia was pulled into it, and the rest of the party joined hands and
followed. Session ends there.

## Key events
- **The third wolf:** Naia found a third set of dire wolf tracks that stop
  mid-lunge, as if the wolf vanished while jumping at the party. Detect
  Magic found nothing.
- **River camp:** Tav built an owlbear-noise gadget; Rowan threw it into
  the camp (it played two owlbears mating, then a stink bomb when Seamus
  stabbed it). Naia swam the river, which was flowing *uphill, away from*
  [[oakhaven|Oakhaven]], and stole two swords, a crossbow, and a coin purse.
  The men later said the river was fine, and by then it was flowing
  normally. Tav repaired their torn map of Oakhaven. The party left
  on Naia's Shape Water raft just as Seamus noticed the theft.
- **Time weirdness:** the dead wolves and their tracks had vanished from the
  fight site. Tav noticed his footsteps' sound arriving late. Tav
  incinerated two crows with Firebolt, and when a crossbow bolt hit the
  third, it burst into feathers with no body.
- **The watchtower:** found on a cliff overlooking Oakhaven, with the kids'
  tracks leading up to it. Three treasure hunters were already there
  ([[varus|Varus]], [[sarah|"Sarah"]], [[dunkirk|Dunkirk]]), two of them
  the pair seen bullying a wagon driver about the watchtower in Session 1.
  Rowan climbed the outside, and the trio left rather than go in.
- **Inside:** an ancient storage room (rope, ~44 gp, two healing potions, a
  journal, and a metal piece marked with the same script as Tav's Rune
  Thing™), then living quarters with star maps. Journal: the stars were
  different, "humming a weird melody," and "this treasure is taking a toll
  on me."
- **The basin:** Tav's Rune Thing™ pulsed toward a marked stone and opened
  stairs to a rune-ringed basement with a big stone head and a basin of
  water dripping *upward*, with something shining in it. Naia reached in
  and was sucked through. The others joined hands and followed.
