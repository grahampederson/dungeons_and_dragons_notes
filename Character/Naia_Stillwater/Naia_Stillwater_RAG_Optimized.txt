## Monk Features and Traits
### Martial Arts
While unarmed or wielding only Monk weapons, and while not wearing armor or wielding a Shield:
- Naia can make an Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action.
- Naia can roll 1d6 instead of the normal damage die for Unarmed Strikes or Monk weapons.
- Naia can use Dexterity instead of Strength for attack and damage rolls with Unarmed Strikes and Monk weapons.
- For the Grapple or Shove option of an Unarmed Strike, Naia can use Dexterity instead of Strength to determine the save DC.

### Unarmored Defense
While not wearing armor or wielding a Shield, Naia's base AC is 14.

### Monk's Focus
Naia has 2 Focus Points. Expended Focus Points are regained after a Short or Long Rest.
- Flurry of Blows: Spend 1 Focus Point to make two Unarmed Strikes as a Bonus Action.
- Patient Defense: Take Disengage as a Bonus Action; or spend 1 Focus Point to take both Disengage and Dodge as a Bonus Action.
- Step of the Wind: Take Dash as a Bonus Action; or spend 1 Focus Point to take both Disengage and Dash as a Bonus Action, and double jump distance for the turn.
- Focus save DC: 8 + Wisdom modifier + Proficiency Bonus.

### Unarmored Movement
Naia's speed increases by 10 ft. while not wearing armor or wielding a Shield.

### Uncanny Metabolism
Once per Long Rest, when Naia rolls Initiative, she can regain all expended Focus Points and regain 1d6 + 2 HP.

## Water Genasi Traits
- Constitution score increases by 2.
- Wisdom score increases by 1.
- Acid Resistance: resistance to acid damage.
- Amphibious: can breathe air and water.
- Swim: swimming speed of 30 feet.
- Call to the Wave: knows the Shape Water cantrip and can cast Create or Destroy Water (2nd) once per Long Rest. Constitution is the spellcasting ability.

### Bonus Actions
- Flurry of Blows: Spend 1 Focus Point to make two Unarmed Strikes as a Bonus Action.
- Patient Defense: Take Disengage as a Bonus Action.

- Step of the Wind: Take Dash as a Bonus Action.
