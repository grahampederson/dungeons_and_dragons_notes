# Naia Stillwater

## Identity

- **Race/Class:** Water Genasi Monk, Way of the Open Hand
- **Descriptive core:** Vibrant, Serene, Meticulous
- **Nickname (party):** "The Leak" (see: Introduction)

## Backstory

### Home — The Cistern Monastery, Salt Marsh of Vell

Born to a family of coastal barge traders; parents died in a storm when Naia was 11. Washed up near the Cistern Monastery, a monastery built into a half-submerged Roman-style cistern in the marsh. Raised there by the monks.

- **Master Ithren** — eldest teacher, unsentimental, gave Naia her name after her parents' names were lost with the boat.
- **Coen Ashgrove** — fellow initiate, clumsy and loud, taught her to laugh again after the storm.
- The monastery floods and drains with the tides — meditation halls are sometimes underwater, sometimes dry stone.
- **What she misses:** the quiet half-hour right after the tide recedes.
- **Recognizable anywhere:** the irregular _tok...tok...tok_ rhythm of water dripping into the cistern's collection basins.

### Defining event — The night the cistern flooded wrong

Age 16. Worst storm surge in a generation. Coen got pinned under a collapsed beam in the flooding lower hall. Naia went back in and got him out — not through calm, disciplined technique, but raw panic and refusal to let go. Ithren afterward: _"That wasn't your training. That was you."_

**Core belief (possibly wrong):** Stillness is a choice you make, not a state you're given — anyone can learn calm under pressure through sufficient discipline. **[Secret]** She doesn't see the scaffolding under her own composure (years of training + a monastery that caught her when she broke) — she thinks it's pure discipline. Makes her occasionally judgmental of party members who panic or freeze, before she catches herself.

### What made her leave

A year after the flood, a merchant sold salvage matching her family's barge nameplate — not proof, but enough to unravel a decade of avoidance. Realized she knew almost nothing about how her parents actually died. Ithren didn't stop her — told her the marsh taught her everything it could about controlling water, but nothing about the ocean that took her parents.

**What she's hoping for:** surface goal — find out what happened to her parents. **[Secret]** Underlying goal — testing whether her belief that discipline can hold against anything actually survives real chaos.

## Personality

### Hobbies / Quirks

- **Can't ignore a bad drip, leak, or misaligned water feature** — will drift toward fixing one mid-conversation, even mid-crisis. Good running bit / DM hook for clues or comic relief.
- Sketches ripple patterns and wave geometry in a battered, water-stained notebook. Mostly circles. Slightly embarrassed if someone pages through it.
- Two weeks free = finds a lake/river and "trains" there (swims, forms, one full day just watching water). Would deny it's a vacation.

### Flaws / What gets her into trouble

- **Core flaw:** mistakes control for safety; sacrifices a lot to preserve the illusion of control. Freezes or overcorrects violently when things go genuinely out of control (vs. controllable chaos, which she handles fine).
- **Bad at:** reading deception/social misdirection — trained to read bodies (stance, breath) not faces or lies. Easily conned by smooth talkers.
- **Break the law:** would break into private property/bribe/threaten for records or salvage rights tied to her parents' wreck.
- **Pick a fight:** anyone echoing Sera Voss's "your calm is just fear in disguise" accusation hits a real nerve.
- **Risk her life:** any drowning/flooding/trapped-underwater scenario — replaying Coen's beam collapse.
- **Betray someone:** might side with Ithren/monastery secrecy over a party member's mission if it came to a direct conflict.
- **Emotionally-driven decision:** would abandon a smarter plan to save Sera Voss mid-rivalry, muttering the whole time about how dumb it is.
- **Impossible risk:** would dive into open ocean (the thing that killed her parents) for Coen or a party member who's earned that trust.

### What she's afraid she'll never experience **[Secret]**

An ordinary day that doesn't require earning it — every day since the storm has felt like something to survive or prove herself worthy of. Suspects her stillness is only ever load-bearing, never restful, and that there's no "off-duty self" underneath it worth knowing. Also afraid she'll never be known by anyone the way the pre-armor people (Coen, Ithren) knew her. Would deflect if asked directly — claims heights or losing an important fight as her "fear" instead.

### What would make her life feel worth living **[Secret]**

Not closure about her parents specifically — she wants to feel one real, uncontained emotion without immediately converting it into breath-work and composure. The real marker: someone seeing her completely fall apart, not managed or performing recovery, and staying anyway. She'd never phrase it this way out loud — she'd say she wants peace, closure, or to honor her parents' memory.

## Calming lines (for agitated party members)

Clinical/diagnostic tone, never warm — delivered like a medic calling out triage, not comfort:

1. "Your breathing's shallow. That'll slow your arm before it slows your nerve."
2. "You're gripping that hilt like it's going to run. It's not going anywhere. Neither are you, yet."
3. "Whatever you're about to say — say it after your shoulders drop. It'll land better."
4. "Panic's just a decision you haven't noticed you're making yet. You can unmake it."
5. "I've watched water do exactly this — churn, go loud, go nowhere. Then it finds a line through. You will too."
6. "You don't need to be calm. You need to be still enough to see straight. Different thing."
7. "Count the drips. There's always something dripping somewhere. Focus on it a moment."
8. "That's fear talking faster than the situation actually requires."
9. "Whatever's coming, it's coming at the same speed either way. Shouting won't hurry it or slow it down."
10. "You're not wrong to be afraid. You're just early. Save it for when it's useful."

## Introduction to the party

Setting: newly formed party at a crossroads tavern/waystation. Naia is found crouched near a leaking barrel/pipe, absorbed in fixing the drip rhythm, notebook open on her knee. Gets pulled into the job almost by accident when someone asks if she's looking for work or snaps at her to weigh in on the argument they're having.

Sample opening line: _"That drip's uneven because the seam's warped, not because the barrel's cracked — someone patched it wrong. ...Sorry. You were saying something about a job?"_

States her reason for joining plainly (needs coin, needs direction, "following something — not sure yet if it's a person or a feeling"). **[Secret]** Real reason: restlessness and an unresolved wound she's finally decided to walk toward.

**DM hook:** If the job routes near water, Naia quietly volunteers to scout that route first — before anyone's told her why she might want to.
