# **Rowan Mercer**

## **Character**

* Name: Rowan Mercer  
* Age: 27  
* Race: Human  
* Class: Fighter  
* Character concept: The Collector

## **Appearance**

Rowan is an athletic, sturdy young man with a friendly face, slightly messy brown hair, and short stubble. He dresses like a well-traveled adventurer, favoring practical clothes, sturdy boots, and a weathered travel coat rather than traditional heavy armor. His chain mail is worn beneath his outer layers, giving him protection without making him look like a knight.

He carries a greatsword across his back and keeps a small pouch of personal keepsakes on his belt. The pouch contains little objects connected to people he's met and experiences he's had—coins, buttons, tokens, and other odds and ends.

## **Personality**

Rowan is adventurous, curious, and easygoing. He enjoys meeting people, hearing their stories, and having new experiences.

He's naturally friendly and interested in people. He enjoys learning what makes people tick and tends to form connections wherever he goes. He's usually willing to follow an interesting opportunity, even when it gets him into trouble.

Rowan has a habit of collecting small objects connected to people he meets and experiences he has—a coin, a button, a token, a broken piece of equipment, or whatever happens to remind him of the moment. They're not valuable. The people and the experiences are what matter.

**Core belief:** *Nobody does anything great alone.*

**Signature phrase:** “Oh yeah, I know a guy.”

## **Backstory**

Rowan grew up in **Bellweather**, a small crossroads town where travelers, merchants, and adventurers regularly passed through. His parents, **Mara and Edric Mercer**, owned the family inn, so Rowan spent his childhood surrounded by people from all over. He loved hearing their stories, learning about the places they'd been, and getting to know the people behind the stories.

When Rowan was sixteen, a violent storm swept through Bellweather and stranded a group of travelers at the inn. What started as a miserable night turned into one of the best nights of Rowan's life. The inn was packed with adventurers, sailors, merchants, and gamblers, and Rowan spent the night talking, laughing, playing games, and helping around the inn.

There was one person in particular Rowan remembers having a particularly great time with. The strange part is that the next morning, Rowan couldn't remember them at all. He couldn't remember their name, their face, where they were from, or what they'd talked about. He only knew they'd been there and that they'd shared a great night. Rowan never figured out what happened, but the experience stayed with him.

After that, Rowan started keeping small things connected to the people he met and the experiences he shared with them. A coin, a button, a token, or a broken piece of equipment could become a reminder of someone or a moment he didn't want to forget. Somewhere along the way, Rowan noticed an old coin in his collection that he didn't remember acquiring. He has no idea where it came from or why he kept it. For now, it's just another odd little piece of his collection.

Over the years, his collection grew along with his connections. Growing up around so many different people also meant Rowan was constantly picking up useful things from them, and before long he had become someone who seemed to know a guy for almost anything.

One of those people was **Garrick Vale**, an experienced adventurer who regularly stayed at the inn. Garrick became Rowan's mentor and taught him what it meant to live as an adventurer—how to travel, prepare for the unexpected, and handle himself on the road. Rowan also discovered a natural talent for fighting. Garrick recognized it and helped him develop it, giving Rowan another way to handle situations when things couldn't be solved by talking.

As Rowan grew older, the stories he'd heard at the inn never stopped fascinating him. He'd heard tales of impossible places, strange creatures, lost civilizations, and legendary treasures. Some were probably true. Some were definitely bullshit. Rowan wanted to know which was which. Eventually, he realized he didn't want to spend his life listening to other people's adventures. He wanted to experience them himself and become part of the kind of stories he'd grown up hearing.

So Rowan left Bellweather to see the world. He doesn't know exactly what he'll find, and that's part of the appeal. He wants to experience the extraordinary things people only hear about in stories and tall tales—and someday be able to say, **“Yeah. I was there.”**

## **Character Questionnaire**

### **1\. Where does your character feel like they belong?**

The family inn in Bellweather. It's where Rowan grew up and where he has always felt at home.

**Small thing from home:**  
The sound of the bell above the front door.

### **2\. What made you into the person you are?**

Growing up around travelers taught Rowan to be curious, listen to people, and make connections. Forgetting someone he shared a great night with made him start collecting little reminders of the people he met and the experiences they shared. Hearing their stories also made him want to see the extraordinary things he'd spent his life hearing about. 

### **3\. What makes your character happy?**

Meeting interesting people, hearing their stories, playing games, good food and drink, and having memorable experiences.

**Two weeks with nothing to do:**  
Travel somewhere he's never been and spend the time meeting people and seeing where the days take him.

**Hobby/quirk:**  
Collecting small objects tied to people and experiences.

### **4\. Who are important people?**

**Someone Rowan loves:**  
**Mara Mercer** — Rowan's mother and the person he's closest to. She is someone Rowan knows he can always come home to.

**Someone who pushes Rowan to become better:**  
**Garrick Vale** — An experienced adventurer who became Rowan's mentor and taught him how to adventure.

**Someone Rowan has conflict with:**  
**Calder Holt** — A longtime friend from Bellweather. Calder is practical and cautious and often challenges Rowan's tendency to follow interesting opportunities without thinking through the consequences.

### **5\. Why did your character become an adventurer?**

Rowan grew up hearing stories about extraordinary places, creatures, treasures, and adventures. Eventually, he wanted to experience those things himself instead of just hearing about them. 

**Who taught him?**  
Garrick Vale.

**What does Rowan hope to accomplish?**  
Rowan wants to discover the extraordinary things people only hear about in stories and experience them firsthand, and become part of those stories himself.

### **6\. What are your character's weaknesses?**

Rowan tends to follow interesting opportunities without always thinking through the consequences. His curiosity and willingness to see where things lead can sometimes get him into trouble.

### **7\. What would truly matter to your character?**

To have experienced the extraordinary, met good people along the way, and have a few great stories of his own. Rowan doesn't need to become famous or legendary. He just wants to know he lived the kind of life he used to hear about at the inn.
