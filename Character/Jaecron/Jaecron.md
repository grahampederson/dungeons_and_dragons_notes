1. Where does your character feel like they belong?

  

Tell me about the place your character considers home. This doesn't necessarily have to be where they were born.

  

Who are the people, places, or experiences that made it feel like home? What does your character miss about it when they're away?

  

Bonus: What is one small, ordinary thing from home that your character would immediately recognize anywhere?

  

2. What made you into the person you are?

  

What experiences shaped your character's personality and worldview?

  

This could include a traumatic event, an important childhood experience, a person who influenced them, or something they strongly believe about the world.

  

Also tell me: What is one belief your character holds strongly—even if that belief might be completely wrong?

  

3. What makes your character happy?

  

What do they do for fun when they aren't adventuring? What hobbies, interests, or little things make them happy?

  

If they suddenly had two weeks with nothing they had to do, how would they spend it?

  

Give me at least one hobby, interest, habit, or quirk that could naturally come up during the campaign.

  

4. Who are the important people in their life?

  

Give me three people:

  

Someone they love or deeply care about.

Someone who pushes them to become better. This could be a friend, rival, mentor, sibling, etc.

Someone they have a history of conflict with.

  

For each, give me their name, relationship, personality, and a brief description of what happened between them.

  

They don't necessarily need to still be alive or involved in your character's life.

  

5. Why did they become an adventurer?

  

Why did your character learn their class? Who taught them the basics, and what was that relationship like?

  

Most importantly:

  

What finally made them leave their normal life behind and risk their life as an adventurer?

  

What are they hoping to find, accomplish, prove, escape, protect, or become?

  

6. What are your character's weaknesses?

  

What flaw, fear, temptation, belief, or personality trait tends to get them into trouble?

  

Describe a situation that could realistically make your character:

  

Break the law.

Pick a fight they shouldn't.

Risk their life.

Betray someone.

Make an emotionally-driven decision.

Or take an impossible risk for someone they care about.

  

Also describe one thing your character is bad at, even if they're otherwise talented.

  

7. What would truly matter to your character?

  

Forget about saving the world for a moment.

  

If your character could have one thing by the end of their life, what would make them feel like they had lived a life worth living?

  

It could be something grand—fame, wealth, revenge, becoming a hero—or something simple, like having a family, finding somewhere to belong, making their parents proud, or simply being remembered.

  

Then answer:

  

What is something your character is afraid they will never get to experience?

  

This can be something they would never openly admit to the rest of the party.
